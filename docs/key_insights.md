# Key Business Insights

## Project: Retail Customer Behavior & Sales Intelligence

This document summarizes the main findings visible in the Power BI report. Values are based on the report and may be rounded.

## 1. Purchase Amount by Category

| Category | Recorded purchase amount |
|---|---:|
| Clothing | $104,264 |
| Accessories | $74,200 |
| Footwear | $36,093 |
| Outerwear | $18,524 |
| **Total** | **$233,081** |

**Finding:** Clothing has the highest recorded purchase amount, while Outerwear has the lowest.

**Potential business action:** Investigate which products contribute most to Clothing's performance and whether Outerwear needs additional product-level analysis.

## 2. Subscription Status

- Subscribed: approximately 27%
- Not subscribed: approximately 73%

**Finding:** Most customer records are associated with non-subscribers.

**Potential business action:** Investigate subscription benefits, customer awareness, and the preferences of existing subscribers before developing a subscription-conversion strategy.

## 3. Average Purchase Amount by Gender

| Gender | Average purchase amount |
|---|---:|
| Female | $60.25 |
| Male | $59.54 |

**Finding:** The reported average purchase amounts are close, with a difference of $0.71.

**Potential business action:** Compare product-category preferences and customer counts before making marketing decisions based on gender.

## 4. Average Purchase Amount by Category

The dashboard reports approximately:

- Footwear: $60.26
- Clothing: $60.03
- Accessories: $59.84
- Outerwear: $57.17

**Finding:** Outerwear has the lowest average purchase amount among the four categories.

**Potential business action:** Investigate item mix, pricing, and customer preferences to understand this difference. The dashboard alone cannot identify its cause.

## 5. Customer Age Groups

The dashboard's largest age group is 56–70, with 1,105 records. The 18–25 group has 571 records.

**Finding:** The distribution of customer records differs across age groups.

**Potential business action:** Compare category preferences and average purchase amounts by age group to understand whether product interests vary across demographics.

## 6. Shopping Preferences

The report compares purchase frequency, discount status, shipping type, and payment methods.

**Finding:** These fields provide useful dimensions for exploring customer preferences, but the dashboard does not establish that any one preference causes higher spending.

**Potential business action:** Compare average purchase amount and customer-record counts across these groups, while checking that the measures and filters are applied consistently.

## Important Interpretation Notes

- Total recorded purchase amount is $233,081.
- Average recorded purchase amount is $59.76.
- The report contains 3,900 customer records.
- The available fields do not include transaction dates, so the analysis should not claim month-by-month or year-by-year sales growth.
- Purchase amount is not the same as profit.
- Differences between groups are descriptive and should be validated before being used to make business decisions.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides an overview of customer demographics, category preferences, subscription participation, and shopping behavior. Clothing contributes the largest recorded purchase amount, subscription participation is comparatively low, and average purchase amounts are similar across genders. Further analysis could investigate category-level preferences, subscription engagement, and customer behavior across demographic groups.
